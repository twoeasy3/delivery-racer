  cleared 11.9 GB from eight private folders; one agent cleaned its own (5.5 GB). Free at the break: about
  29 GB of 475.

  `menu-tmp` 6.3 GB, `tmp` 5.1 GB, `tmp-evil` 1.1 GB, `tmp-g3` 0.5 GB, `tmp-agent11` 0.2 GB of browser
